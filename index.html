Who We Are

The Space is a real estate project management company based in Tbilisi. We work with both individuals and businesses to plan, coordinate, and deliver property projects of any scale — from a single apartment renovation to a full commercial fit-out.
Rather than hiring separate architects, contractors, suppliers, and managers, our clients have one team handling everything. We take full responsibility for the project from the first site visit to the final walkthrough, so you can focus on what matters to you.

Mission

To be the most trusted partner for real estate project management in Georgia — making complex projects simple, transparent, and stress-free for every client.

Vision

A Georgia where individuals and businesses can realise any property vision without the complexity — because we handle it all.

What We Offer

Managing a real estate project involves dozens of moving parts — architects, builders, suppliers, inspectors, and more. We coordinate all of them under one roof, so nothing falls through the cracks and you always know where your project stands.

Getting Started

Starting is simple. Here's what happens when you get in touch:

  1. Reach out by phone, email, social media, or visit our office in Tbilisi. Tell us about your project — there's no obligation.

  2. We arrange a convenient time to visit the site. Our representative assesses the space and listens to your goals in detail.

  3. We prepare a clear, itemised quote — no vague estimates, no surprise costs. Completely free.

  4. Once we agree on the scope and timeline, we take it from there. You stay informed at every stage; we handle the rest.
